Khoa

Hi again! I'm a Unity gameplay programmer who gets way too excited about clean architecture. I build scalable systems, modular code and maintainable C# that doesn't turn into spaghetti.

I love designing unique mechanics and extensible tools, and making systems that stay flexible as a project grows.
